# Model Card: pod-failure-prediction

## Model Overview

Gradient boosting classifier that estimates whether a pod will fail within the next
hour, trained on Feast pod-metric features. Used by the UC1 retrain DAG.

## Intended Use

- Primary use: pre-production and production pod-failure alerting inside this platform.
- Out of scope: any decision that scales or evicts workloads without the OPA gate.

## Performance

Holdout metrics are logged to MLflow on every retrain (`accuracy`, `f1_score`, `roc_auc`).
A promotion requires accuracy ≥ 0.70, drift PSI ≤ 0.10, and a Model Trust Score ≥ 700
for production.

## Data Governance

Training data is `feast/pod_metrics_features`, generated by the seeded synthetic
pod-metrics pipeline and tracked with OpenLineage from the retrain DAG.

## Risk

This is a high-risk operations model: a false negative misses a failing pod, a false
positive can trigger an unnecessary remediation. Mitigations:

- drift monitor (UC1) retrains when PSI exceeds 0.10
- OPA blocks production promotion below the trust bar
- human approval is required to move the MLflow alias to production
- rollback is implemented in `platform/rollback/auto_rollback.py`

## Limitations

Trained on synthetic pod metrics until a production feature snapshot is wired in.
SHAP explanations are added by the UC17 workflow; until that tag is present the
EU AI Act checker reports the model non-compliant and the trust score cannot reach PRIME.
